\section*{Ethics statement}
The study used only publicly available, de-identified fundus photographs released for research
(IDRiD, Messidor-2, APTOS 2019, EyePACS 2015, DDR, DeepDRiD and SUSTech-SYSU). No new data were
collected from human participants, and ethical approval was not required.

\section*{Data and code availability}
All images are from public datasets. Code, the frozen splits, per-image out-of-fold and external
predictions for every model, the analysis plans with their commit history, and every generated
table and figure are available at
\url{https://github.com/Ahmadrezanourozii/Automatic-detection-of-diabetic-retinopathy-and-grading-of-diabetic-macular-edema-using-CNN}.
